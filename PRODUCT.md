# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single active trader reviewing a personal trade journal between or after trading sessions. The primary job is to identify whether realised results, risk, instruments, and sessions are helping or hurting performance without asking for extra tracking work.

## Product Purpose

Turn the trades already recorded in the journal into a compact, trustworthy performance review. Success means the user can understand the period's P&L, R expectancy, downside, and meaningful ticker/session differences at a glance.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, and precise. Dark and data-forward, with emphasis on signal over ceremony.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified scores, dense analytics walls, process-compliance workflows, decorative data visualizations, and metrics that look authoritative but have insufficient data.

## Design Principles

1. Show the few decisions that can change the next trade review.
2. Make data provenance and sample size legible.
3. Let empty states teach the required data rather than simulate activity.
4. Prefer stable, familiar dashboard affordances over novelty.
5. Keep the journal low-friction: no manual rules or process tracking required.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast where practical, communicate gain/loss with words and signs as well as color, support reduced motion, and keep tables usable with local horizontal scrolling.
